About

Technology built around real people.

I am a Mechatronics Engineering student passionate about building technology that solves real-world problems. My work combines mobile development, artificial intelligence, accessibility and cloud-based software.

I have developed applications for iOS, Android and the web, participated in award-winning hackathon projects and published an application on the Apple App Store. I enjoy working across the full product lifecycle, from the initial idea and interface design to implementation, testing and deployment.

My current interests include AI-assisted applications, accessibility-focused products, computer vision, local AI inference, mobile development and scalable cloud services.

How I approach product development

  1. 01
    Understand the problem

    Start with people, context and the outcome the product needs to support.

  2. 02
    Build the complete flow

    Connect interface, application logic, services, testing and deployment.

  3. 03
    Design for access

    Consider clarity, adaptability and inclusive interaction from the beginning.
